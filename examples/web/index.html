or click to choose. MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and OGG, whatever your browser can play.
How it worksClassic music analysis, no models to download: onset autocorrelation for tempo, chroma templates for chords, Krumhansl profiles for key, a self-similarity matrix for sections.
What it gets rightSteady-tempo pop, rock, folk and worship songs with major and minor chords. Around three in four chords on a typical mix.
What it gets wrongSevenths, inversions and jazz voicings come back as plain triads. Rubato and tempo changes confuse the beat grid. Half or double tempo happens; the buttons fix it.

chordmap is open source (MIT). The audio is decoded and analyzed on this device and is never uploaded. There is no analytics, no account and no server.

Chords are major and minor triads only. Section names like "chorus" are guesses from repetition and loudness. Check the chart against your ears.
