Chordmap for Mac. Free, open source.
Play any song on the Mac and see the chords bar by bar, the key, the tempo, and where the capo goes so the open shapes play it. Every listen kept with its chord sheet.
It hears; it never records, and it does not know the song's name. Rust engine, nothing uploaded.
https://github.com/keithadler/chordmap
