Chordmap for Mac. Free, open source.

Play any song on the Mac and see the chords as they go by: the key, the tempo, the chords bar by bar, the sections, and where the capo goes so the open shapes play it. Eb major becomes capo 3, play in C.

It hears; it never records, and it does not know the song's name. A clear mix charts well, a wall of distortion less well, and sparse harmony is flagged, not invented.

Every listen is kept with its chord sheet to copy. A guitar into an interface works too. Also a command line: chordmac song.wav --capo 3

The engine is chordmap, pure Rust, the same one behind the web app at keithadler.github.io/chordmap.
